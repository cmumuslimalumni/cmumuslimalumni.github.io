Assalamu Alaikum
 

Welcome — however you found your way here, we’re really glad you did.

MAN @ CMU launched this spring during Carnival, out of a simple idea: graduating from CMU shouldn’t mean losing the community you built while you were there. With the new school year almost here, we’re ready to get a lot more active — more events, more ways to find each other, more reasons to stay in each other’s corner.

If you’re in the DMV, come say salaam in person at our first social on August 13th — we’d love to meet you. And whether you’ve got a role to share, a story worth featuring, or just want to say hello, we want to hear from you.

Alumni Spotlight
 
 
Sumayya Syeda
ECE & Robotics, c/o [20XX] · Software Engineer at Apple · San Diego

Sumayya is a Systems & Performance Engineer on Apple’s Streaming Media team, working deep in the infrastructure that keeps video playing smoothly for hundreds of millions of people who never think about it once.

That’s a long way from where she started. Robotics and ECE point most people toward hardware, and she came out of CMU expecting to build things you could touch. Instead she found she loved the layer just underneath the screen — the systems and performance work nobody sees until it breaks — and followed that curiosity from campus robotics labs into some of the lowest-level, highest-stakes software at Apple.

Her advice: “Network everywhere you go, because you will never know when an opportunity might show up simply from taking the time to be kind and say hi to someone you respect.”
