Assalamu Alaikum
 

Welcome to the first edition of the Muslim Alumni Network @ Carnegie Mellon University newsletter. We’re really glad you’re here.

Since officially launching during Carnival in April, we’ve been spending the past few months laying the groundwork for what we hope will become a lasting community for Muslim CMU alumni. A lot of work has been happening quietly behind the scenes – reconnecting with alumni, figuring out how to sustainably support the network, and reaching out to organizations and partners who share our vision. We’ve also developed a strong partnership with the university and hope to leverage that relationship to strengthen Muslim life on campus as we continue building out the network.

We’ve been working to build stronger connections between the broader Muslim community at CMU and beyond. That includes conversations with the CMU MSA and Centers for Muslim Life, exploring partnerships with organizations like NI’MA and other Muslim alumni networks, and planning events and initiatives that bring our community together.

Ultimately, we believe in a simple idea: to connect with alumni old and new and build a community that outlasts graduation, inshallah. We want MAN@CMU to not only be a space where alumni come together, but also a space where we can mobilize to strengthen Muslim life at CMU and support current students. A space to share resources, lean on each other, and grow together is exactly what this ummah needs right now.

This newsletter will serve as one of the ways we keep you in the loop – from upcoming events and community initiatives to opportunities to get involved and ways you can support the next generation of Muslim Tartans.

As we get to know one another through this network, we hope you’ll stick around, give back when you can, and help us build something that lasts. We’re really glad you’re part of it. If you haven’t already, please follow us on IG and LinkedIn, and let us know how you’d like to be involved. And feel free to reach out if you have ideas to share or would like to contribute to our efforts.

JZK for being here. We’re excited for what’s ahead, inshallah.

— The MAN @ CMU Board

Sumayya Syeda
Core Board · ECE & Robotics, c/o 2024
Software Engineer at Apple · San Diego
 

Sumayya is a Systems & Performance Engineer on Apple’s Streaming Media team, working deep in the infrastructure that keeps video playing smoothly for hundreds of millions of people who never think about it once.

Sumayya was deeply involved with the MSA as a student, eventually serving on its board. She loved the chance to give back to the community — creating a space for Muslims to gather, socialize, and network, and most importantly, a safe space where students could connect. After graduating, she wanted to keep giving back to the community that had given her so much support, which is what drew her to help build something that connects past and present students.

Her advice: “Network everywhere you go, because you will never know when an opportunity might show up simply from taking the time to be kind and say hi to someone you respect.”

Reach Sumayya at sumsyeda7@gmail.com or on LinkedIn.
